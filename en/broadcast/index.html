What does our broadcast service include?

We support every project end to end: from design and equipment importing through commissioning and ongoing maintenance, with in-house engineering and lifecycle support.

TV studios and control rooms

We design and integrate television studios, control rooms and editing and transmission systems, sized to each client’s workflow.

IT infrastructure and connectivity

We deploy IT infrastructure and resilient connectivity for networks, universities and government bodies that cannot afford to go off air.

Media asset management (MAM)

We integrate cataloging, archiving and media asset management systems, including AI-powered solutions that speed up production.

Live video transmission

We deploy live transmission and distribution solutions, including cellular bonding technology, to broadcast from anywhere with professional quality.

Technical support and installations

Our field engineering team handles installation and the preventive and corrective maintenance needed to keep operations running continuously.

Importing and compliance

We manage equipment imports with logistical traceability and regulatory compliance, and take part in public and private tenders.
