About us

What is La Curaçao?

A company of Dutch origin that began operations in Colombia in 1919 under the name Curaçao Trading Company.

In 1962 it was incorporated as Compañía Comercial Curacao de Colombia S.A. (La Curaçao), a subsidiary of the Dutch group CETECO. Since 1990 it has operated as a 100% Colombian company, with a collegiate legal and managerial structure.

What does a century of continuity mean?

It is not just a number to celebrate. For anyone procuring critical technology, a supplier's longevity has concrete consequences.

Financial stability

Operating without interruption for more than a hundred years, through several national crises, is itself proof of solidity.

Regulatory experience

A century importing technical equipment leaves a knowledge of customs and public procurement that cannot be improvised.

Long-term relationships

Exclusive representations are earned through years of delivery. Several of the brands we represent have trusted the company for decades.

Support that is still there

A critical system lasts years. Hiring a century-old company reduces the risk of being left without support halfway through its lifecycle.
