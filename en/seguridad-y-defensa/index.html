What does our defense unit include?

We specialize in the procurement, deployment and integration of tactical technology solutions for defense organizations, public safety and emergency response.

Strategic and tactical communications

Satellite, HF, VHF, UHF and wireless systems to keep the link up in operations where communication cannot fail.

Cyberdefense and cybersecurity

Protection for the digital infrastructure of organizations operating in critical environments, where an incident compromises the mission.

Monitoring and lawful interception

Surveillance, control and management systems with the compliance framework required by state intelligence and security operations.

Protective equipment

High-performance eye protection, medical supplies for wound care and armament, with the certifications each application requires.

Operational and forensic software

Forensic image and video analysis tools for investigation, law enforcement and operational decision support.

Rapid field deployment

Portable mast and rapid-deployment systems to elevate communications, cameras and sensors in tactical operations.
