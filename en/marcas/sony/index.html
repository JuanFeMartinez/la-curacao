The SONY professional portfolio

SONY covers the entire professional audiovisual production chain, from capture through display and archiving. These are the product families we integrate and support.

Digital cinema cameras

The VENICE line, with full-frame sensor, X-OCN recording and wide dynamic range, alongside the FX series for cinematic production.

Studio and broadcast cameras

Studio and outside-broadcast camera chains with 4K, HDR and high frame rate support for live and sports production.

XDCAM camcorders

Handheld and shoulder camcorders for ENG and field production, such as the PXW-Z200 with 20x optical zoom, BIONZ XR processing and 12G-SDI output.

PTZ and remote cameras

Motorised pan, tilt and zoom cameras that cover multiple angles with a single operator, with controllers such as the RM-IP500.

Switchers and live production

XVS and MVS series production switchers, IP live production systems, NS-BUS routing and newsroom automation.

Monitors, displays and archiving

Professional reference monitors, displays and LED panels, plus storage, optical archiving and content management solutions.
