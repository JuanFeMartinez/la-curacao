We integrate and support LiveU solutions in Colombia

LiveU is a world leader in live video transmission and distribution. Its cellular bonding technology, built on the proprietary LRT (LiveU Reliable Transport) protocol, aggregates multiple network connections into a single stable link to broadcast live from anywhere with professional quality.

What do we represent from LiveU?

As LiveU partners, we integrate and support their live transmission solutions: bonding units, stream management and video distribution for sectors such as corporate, government, media and events in Colombia.

The LiveU portfolio

LiveU covers the full live video chain: capturing in the field, producing, managing, distributing and maintaining connectivity. These are the product families we integrate and support.

5G field units

Cellular bonding encoders such as the LU800, LU300S and LU900Q, the Solo PRO backpack and the LU-Smart app, plus the LU810 and LU610 rackmount encoders.

LRT protocol

LiveU Reliable Transport is the proprietary technology that holds the link together over cellular and IP networks, even when network conditions are poor.

Cloud production

LiveU Studio for cloud production, along with IP Pipe for remote gear control, and Video Return and Audio Connect for the feed back to the field.

Management and monitoring

LiveU Central as the cloud management platform, with LiveU Ingest for recording and metadata, LiveU Schedule and the LiveU Nexus gateway.

IP distribution

LiveU Matrix for cloud-native IP video distribution, and LU4000 and LU2000 decoders for reception at the production centre.

Connectivity

Data plans, LiveU DataBridge, LU-LINK and LiveU Xtend to secure the bandwidth each transmission needs.
