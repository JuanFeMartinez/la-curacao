Privacy and personal data policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Compañía Comercial Curacao de Colombia S.A. processes the personal data it receives, in compliance with Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and the other Colombian personal data protection regulations.

1. Data controller

  • Company name: Compañía Comercial Curacao de Colombia S.A.
  • Tax ID (NIT): 860.004.871-7.
  • Address: Diagonal 36 Bis No. 20-74, Parkway de La Soledad, Bogotá D.C., Colombia.
  • Phone: +57 (601) 877 0070.
  • Email: info@curacao.com.co.

2. Data we collect

Through the contact form we receive the name, email address, phone number, company and message that the person chooses to send us. If analytics cookies are accepted, browsing data is also collected in aggregate form, such as the pages visited, the type of device and the approximate location.

We do not request sensitive data or data from children or adolescents.

3. Purposes

  • Responding to requests, inquiries and quotes.
  • Providing commercial and technical follow-up on those requests.
  • Sending information about products and services related to the request received.
  • Measuring how the website is used in order to improve it, when analytics cookies are accepted.
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to requests from competent authorities.

4. Authorization

By checking the authorization box on the form, data subjects give their prior, express and informed authorization for their data to be processed for the purposes described. The authorization may be revoked at any time, except where there is a legal or contractual duty to keep the data.

5. Rights of the data subject

  • To know, update and correct their personal data.
  • To request proof of the authorization granted.
  • To be informed about how their data has been used.
  • To file complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) after completing the inquiry or complaint process with La Curaçao.
  • To revoke the authorization or request the deletion of their data.
  • To access their personal data free of charge.

6. Inquiries and complaints

Inquiries, complaints and requests are received at info@curacao.com.co or at the company's address. The request must include the data subject's name, identity document, a description of the request and contact details for the reply.

  • Inquiries: answered within a maximum of ten (10) business days, which may be extended by five (5) additional business days, stating the reason.
  • Complaints: resolved within a maximum of fifteen (15) business days, which may be extended by eight (8) additional business days, stating the reason.

7. Processors and transfers

To receive and store form messages and for website analytics we use services from Google LLC, which acts as a data processor and may store the information on servers located outside Colombia with adequate levels of protection. We do not sell or transfer personal data to third parties.

8. Security and retention

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect data against loss and unauthorized use or access. Data is kept for as long as necessary for the purposes described and to comply with legal obligations.

9. Cookies

Cookies are small files that the browser stores when you visit a website. This website uses:

  • Technical storage: remembers the cookie choice and whether the welcome screen has already been shown. It does not identify the person and is not shared.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): only activated if the person accepts them. They measure in aggregate how the website is used and may last up to two years.

The choice can be changed at any time from “Cookie preferences” at the bottom of each page, or by deleting cookies in the browser settings.

10. Validity

This policy is effective from its publication date. The databases will remain in force for as long as the purposes described are fulfilled. Any substantial change will be published on this page.
